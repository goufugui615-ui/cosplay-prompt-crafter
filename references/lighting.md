# 光线速查

| 光线 | 关键词（可直接抄） | 适用 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 窗边柔光 | soft window light from camera left, diffused daylight, gentle falloff | 私房、居家、清新 |
| 侧逆光 | side backlight at 45°, rim light on hair, warm glow | 氛围、发丝光 |
| 棚拍柔光箱 | large softbox 45° above subject, beauty dish fill, neutral gray background | 商业、车展、杂志感 |
| 夜景霓虹 | neon signs bokeh background, magenta and cyan practical lights, high ISO look | 赛博、街头 |
| 闪光灯直打 | direct on-camera flash, hard shadows, slightly overexposed highlights | 复古胶片、抓拍 |
| 阴天自然光 | overcast diffused light, flat but even, muted colors | 户外、街拍 |
| 黄昏 | golden hour backlight, long shadows, warm skin tone | 海边、天台 |

**注意**：写"光线好"没用；必须写**方向 + 质感 + 色温**。与镜头配合时，先定光线再定机位。
